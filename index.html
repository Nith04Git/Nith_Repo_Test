Introducing WeatherBox

What is WeatherBox?

WeatherBox is a project aiming to combine weather data from powerful technologies, local on-field sensors and AI functionality to provide precise weather.

What powers WeatherBox?

  • Arduino UNO R4 WiFi
  • DHT11 Temperature & Humidity sensor
  • 7cm*7cm Solar Panel
  • Micro SD Card Reader
  • uBlox Neo6M GPS Module
